Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

What we collect

Your name and email; the URL or repository you ask us to audit or write about; if you choose a repo audit, read-only access to that repo (or an uploaded copy). Payment details are handled by Stripe, our payment processor; we never see full card numbers.

What the audit does

Launch Check loads your public pages like a browser would, checks links, and scans the repo for secrets, env files, database rules and build/deploy configuration. If we find secrets, the report shows only a redacted snippet and its location.

How we use it

Only to produce and deliver the service you bought. We don't sell your data or use your code to train models.

Retention

We delete any repo copy once your report or kit is delivered. We keep your order emails, briefs and deliverables only as long as needed to support your order; email us any time to have them deleted.

Sharing

Only with service providers needed to run the service (hosting, email, payments).

Your rights

Email norcalcracks@gmail.com to access or delete your data.
